## Hi, I am Ioannis

I am Head of Software Engineering at [Cyber Ranges](https://cyberranges.com). Before that, I spent over a decade at [Red Hat](https://www.redhat.com), most recently on the [Quarkus](https://quarkus.io) team.

I have been in this field for more than 21 years, 15 of them working full time in open source. I am a member of the [Apache Software Foundation](https://www.apache.org), and I created projects like [Fabric8 Kubernetes Client](https://github.com/fabric8io/kubernetes-client) and [Spring Cloud Kubernetes](https://github.com/spring-cloud/spring-cloud-kubernetes).

These days I care about how engineering teams work, what makes them effective, and how AI agents are changing the way we build software. For me, simplicity beats sophistication, in teams as much as in code.

This is where I write about all of that, as I have been doing [since the Blogspot days](https://iocanel.blogspot.com). You can also find my [talks](/presentations/), my [projects](/portfolio/) and a bit more [about me](/about/).
